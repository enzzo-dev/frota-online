How sessions work

      

        
15 min
Access token lifetime. It is a signed JWT carrying your id, e-mail and role.

        
30 days
Refresh token lifetime. It is random, shown once, and stored on our side only as a hash.

        
1 use
Every refresh token works once. Using it again is treated as theft and ends the session.

      

      
Roles are Customer, Driver and Admin. Sign-up only creates the first two. Passwords need 8 to 128 characters with an upper case letter, a lower case letter and a digit.

    Driver onboarding
    
      
    A driver cannot send proposals until an admin approves the profile. These are the calls, in order. Authenticate as a Driver for steps 1 to 6 and as an Admin for step 7.
    
      
    
        
    
          
    StepCallWhat happens
    
          
            
    1GET /terms/pending then POST /terms/acceptAccept every active term. Each acceptance stores the time and IP address.
    
            
    2PUT /driver/profileSend taxId (CPF or CNPJ, letters allowed), CNH and category. The tax id is required and checked by its digits before anything is saved. Repeat the call to correct data while the profile is a draft or rejected.
    
            
    3POST /driver/vehiclesRegister at least one vehicle. Plates accept ABC1234 and ABC1D23.
    
            
    4POST /driver/documentsRegister the files you already uploaded. The storage key must start with drivers/{your user id}/.
    
            
    5GET /driver/profileThe checklist shows what is still missing: terms, vehicle, document.
    
            
    6POST /driver/submitMoves the status to PendingReview and locks the profile. An incomplete checklist returns the missing items.
    
            
    7GET /admin/drivers, then approve or rejectThe admin reviews the file. Approval or rejection (with a reason) sends an e-mail. A rejected driver edits and submits again.
    
              
        
    
      
    
      
    In Development the API creates an admin on first start (admin@frotaonline.local, password from Seed:Admin:Password in appsettings.Development.json) and publishes version 1.0 of the terms. Sign in with it to try step 7.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Marketplace
    
      
    The customer posts a freight, approved drivers bid with their own price, and the customer picks one. The platform never touches the freight money.
    
      
    
        
    
          
    StepWhoCallWhat happens
    
          
            
    1CustomerPOST /addressesRegister every pickup and delivery address. State must be a valid UF and the ZIP has 8 digits. Customers can be people or companies.
    
            
    2CustomerPOST /freightsSend stops in the order the driver follows, each one a Pickup or a Delivery with optional items and a note. A freight can have several pickups and several deliveries; the first stop must be a pickup and the last a delivery, the final address. Needs every active term accepted. Without scheduledPickupAt the freight is for today and takes proposals for 5 minutes; a scheduled one takes them for 24 hours (UTC, trailing Z).
    
            
    3DriverGET /driver/freights/openOnly freights whose vehicle type matches one of your active vehicles. Each freight shows how many pickups and deliveries it has and, per stop, the area and the items. Street addresses stay hidden until approval.
    
            
    4DriverPOST /driver/freights/{id}/proposalsPick a vehicle of the right type that carries the weight, and set the price. One pending proposal per freight; withdraw it to change your mind.
    
            
    5CustomerGET /freights/{id}/proposalsPending proposals ranked by price, cheapest first. Ties go to the earliest.
    
            
    6CustomerPOST /freights/{id}/proposals/{proposalId}/approveCreates the delivery, books the driver's agenda (2 hours plus 1 hour per stop, from the first pickup), rejects the other proposals and closes the freight. A driver already booked in that period returns proposal.driver_busy.
    
            
    7DriverGET /driver/deliveries, then GET /driver/deliveries/{id}/routeLists the deliveries you won and opens the route with full addresses. view=Full shows every pickup and delivery at once, as an overview of the run. view=Next shows only the stop to do now, so the app can go one stop after the other. Each stop lists its items.
    
            
    8DriverPOST /driver/deliveries/{id}/startLeaves for the first pickup. Scheduled becomes ToPickup.
    
            
    9DriverPOST /driver/deliveries/{id}/stops/{stopId}/completeCompletes the next stop; stops must be done in order (delivery.stop_out_of_order). The first pickup moves the delivery to PickedUp and InTransit, and the last stop marks it Delivered, accrues the R$ 4.90 fee and frees the agenda. Optional note, coordinates and a DeliveryProof file (register it first with POST /driver/documents).
    
            
    10DriverPOST /driver/deliveries/{id}/incident or /cancelReports an incident (then only cancelling is possible) or cancels while Scheduled, ToPickup or after an incident. The agenda is freed.
    
            
    11CustomerGET /deliveries, GET /deliveries/{id}Follows the run: status, driver and vehicle, stops with completion times, payment status and the event timeline. POST /deliveries/{id}/cancel works only before the cargo is picked up.
    
            
    12DriverPOST /driver/deliveries/{id}/paymentAfter Delivered, confirms the customer paid (Pix, Cash, Card or Other). Once per delivery. The money never passes through the platform.
    
            
    13Driver / AdminGET /driver/fees, GET and POST /admin/drivers/{id}/fees[/settle]The fee ledger: R$ 4.90 accrues per delivered freight and the admin records what the driver paid. A settlement cannot exceed the open balance (fee.exceeds_balance).
    
              
        
    
      
    
      
    A background job runs every 30 seconds and expires freights and proposals past their window. Until it runs, the API already refuses proposals that arrive late.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Privacy and data deletion (LGPD)
    
      
    Every signed-in user can see what the platform holds about them and ask for it to be erased, as the LGPD (art. 18) requires.
    
      
    
        
    
          
    CallWhat happens
    
          
            
    GET /me/dataExports everything held about the user as JSON: account, tax id and CNH, vehicles, addresses, files, accepted terms, freights, proposals and deliveries.
    
            
    POST /me/deletionSend { "password": "...", "confirm": true }. The account is anonymized: name, e-mail, phone, documents, plates and street addresses are erased, files and sessions are deleted, open freights are cancelled and pending proposals closed. The response lists what stays.
    
              
        
    
      
    
      
    Freights, proposals, deliveries and payment records stay without identifying anyone, because the law requires them for audit and tax (LGPD art. 16). Deletion is refused while a delivery is in progress (privacy.active_deliveries) and for administrators. Access tokens already issued last up to 15 minutes, so discard them in the app after deleting. The e-mail becomes free for a new sign-up.

    Errors
    
      
    Every failure comes back as JSON in the problem details format. Read the status code first, then title for a stable code you can branch on.
    
      
    
        
    
          
    A failed request
    
          
    HTTP/1.1 409 Conflict
{
  "title": "auth.email_in_use",
  "detail": "This e-mail is already registered.",
  "status": 409
}
    
        
    
        
    
          
    A request with invalid fields
    
          
    HTTP/1.1 400 Bad Request
{
  "title": "One or more validation errors occurred.",
  "status": 400,
  "errors": {
    "Password": ["Password must contain a digit."]
  }
}
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
    
          
    StatusCodeWhat it means and what to do
    
          
            
    400validation_errorA field is missing or invalid. Read the errors object and fix those fields.
    
            
    401auth.invalid_credentialsWrong e-mail or password. The message is the same for both on purpose.
    
            
    401auth.invalid_refresh_tokenThe refresh token is unknown, expired or already used. Ask the person to sign in again.
    
            
    401no bodyThe access token is missing, invalid or expired. Refresh it and retry once.
    
            
    403auth.user_inactiveThe account is disabled. Contact support.
    
            
    409auth.email_in_useThat e-mail already has an account. Send the person to sign in.
    
            
    409driver.profile_lockedThe profile is under review or approved. It can only be edited while draft or rejected.
    
            
    409driver.tax_id_in_use / driver.cnh_in_use / driver.plate_in_useAnother account already registered that document or plate.
    
            
    400driver.submission_incompleteTerms, vehicle or document is missing. The errors object names each one.
    
            
    403no bodyThe signed-in role cannot use this endpoint, for example a Driver calling /admin.
    
            
    403freight.terms_not_acceptedThe customer must accept the active terms before posting a freight.
    
            
    404freight.not_found, proposal.not_found, delivery.not_foundThe freight, proposal or delivery does not exist or belongs to someone else.
    
            
    409freight.not_open, freight.window_closedThe freight is closed, cancelled, expired or past its proposal window. Reload the list.
    
            
    409proposal.not_pending, proposal.already_submittedThe proposal was already decided, or you already have a pending one on this freight.
    
            
    403proposal.driver_not_approvedOnly approved drivers can send proposals.
    
            
    409proposal.driver_unavailable, proposal.vehicle_unavailable, proposal.driver_busyThe driver or vehicle is inactive, or the driver's agenda is taken at that time. Pick another proposal.
    
            
    400proposal.vehicle_not_eligibleThe vehicle has the wrong type or cannot carry the weight.
    
            
    409privacy.active_deliveriesA delivery is still in progress, so the account cannot be deleted yet.
    
            
    403privacy.admin_not_allowedAdministrator accounts must be removed by another administrator.
    
            
    404privacy.account_not_foundThe account does not exist or was already anonymized.
    
            
    409delivery.invalid_statusThe delivery is not in a status that allows this action.
    
            
    404delivery.stop_not_foundThe stop does not belong to the delivery.
    
            
    409delivery.stop_out_of_orderComplete the earlier stops first.
    
            
    400delivery.invalid_proofThe proof must be a DeliveryProof file registered by the driver.
    
            
    409payment.not_delivered, payment.already_confirmedPayment is confirmed only after delivery, once.
    
            
    409fee.exceeds_balanceThe settlement is higher than the open balance.
    
            
    409request.duplicateTwo identical requests arrived together and the database rejected the second one (unique index), for example the payment confirmation. Reload and check the current state.
